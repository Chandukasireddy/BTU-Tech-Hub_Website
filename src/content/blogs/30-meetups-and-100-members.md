## 29 Meetups, One Community

It started with a single session in October 2025. Since then, BTU Tech Hub has hosted **29 meetups** at the IKMZ cabins, BTU Cottbus-Senftenberg. Some were hands-on builds, some were deep dives into new tools, and some were just a room full of people learning together.

Our biggest session so far was Meetup #26, with **28 registrations** and **20 participants**. Every session has been built by the people who came: the students who asked the questions, shared their projects, and helped others get unstuck.

## 100+ Members on Discord

Our Discord community has now passed **100 members**. That number matters to us, because it means more people finding a place to learn, build, and connect, whether they can make it to a Sunday session or not.

Thank you to everyone who has joined, shared, and shown up.

## Meetup #30: Agent Knowledge

Tomorrow, **Saturday, 7 November 2026 at 12:00 PM** at the IKMZ cabins, we host **Meetup #30**. The session focuses on what makes AI agents trustworthy in practice:

- **Guardrails:** Setting clear limits for what an agent may do, and checking its output before it acts.
- **Agent Harness:** The code around the model that runs the loop, calls tools, and recovers from errors.
- **Skills:** Packaging capabilities into reusable pieces an agent can load when it needs them.
- **LangFuse Monitoring:** Tracing every step of an agent run, so you can see what went wrong and why.

The session is hands-on. Bring a laptop, bring a half-finished idea, and expect to leave with something that runs.
