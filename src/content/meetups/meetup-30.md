## About Event

BTU Tech Hub: 30th Meetup

**Connect • Learn • Build**

Meetup #30 is about **agent knowledge**: how to build AI agents that are safe, reliable, and observable. It is a Saturday session, so come ready to build.

### What We Will Cover

- **Guardrails:** How to keep an agent inside clear boundaries, and how to check its output before it acts.
- **Agent Harness:** The scaffolding around a model that runs its loop, calls tools, and handles failures.
- **Skills:** Reusable, well-defined capabilities an agent can load and apply.
- **LangFuse Monitoring:** Tracing agent runs, inspecting each step, and measuring quality over time.

### What to Expect

- **Hands-on Session:** Bring your laptop and build along.
- **Open Discussion:** Share what you have built and what broke.
- **Networking:** Meet fellow students, developers, and tech enthusiasts at BTU.
